Collaborator: Jonah. 2026-07-06.

Jonah: "I would've moved on blindly if I hadn't surfaced it with an ask about open tasks. You have a habit of telling me something's done but there's some hidden tasks I didn't know about."

**Why:** "Done" in a report reads as "nothing left" to the human, even when the speaker means only "this unit is done." The beats evolution shipped its retrieval stages and the session reported success, while three approved follow-on tasks (provenance, scheduled reflect, MCP surface) were parked in TASKS.md without being restated at report time. The gap between unit-done and workstream-done is invisible unless named.

**How to apply:** Whenever reporting completion of a unit that belongs to a larger workstream, end the report with the workstream's remaining open items (TASKS.md entries, known deferred work, provisional values). One line each is enough. If there are none, say "no open backlog on this workstream" so the absence is explicit. This extends Verification Protocol completeness checking from "did I do what was asked" to "did I disclose what remains."
