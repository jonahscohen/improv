Cordage moves marine cargo

We book container space on the short-sea routes the large forwarders skip, and we quote the full door-to-door price rather than the ocean leg alone.

What we quote

A Cordage quote covers drayage at both ends, the ocean leg, customs entry, and any terminal handling the port charges. The line items are shown separately so you can see where the money goes, and the total is what you are invoiced.

Sailing schedule

We publish the next eight weeks of departures for every lane we serve. If a sailing is cancelled or rolled, the booking moves to the next departure automatically and you are told the same day rather than at the terminal.

Customs

Entries are filed by our own licensed brokers, not a subcontractor. Duty is calculated against the tariff classification on file for your product, and a classification change is reviewed with you before it is used on a live entry.
