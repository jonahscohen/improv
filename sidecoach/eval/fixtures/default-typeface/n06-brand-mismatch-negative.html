Ostinato schedules rehearsal rooms

Twelve rooms, four pianos, and a drum room that is actually isolated. Book by the half hour and let yourself in with the code the booking sends you.

Rooms

Rooms one through six take a quartet comfortably. Seven and eight hold a small ensemble with stands and a conductor's position. The drum room floats on a separate slab, so a full kit at volume does not bleed into the room next door.

Pianos

The two grands are tuned monthly and the uprights every six weeks, with the tuning date posted on the room door. If a piano drifts between visits, flag it in the app and we pull the room from booking until the technician has been.

Access

The building is open from seven in the morning until midnight. Your door code is valid for your booked slot plus ten minutes at each end, which is enough to load in and out without paying for an extra half hour.
