Sherpa routes freight without the phone calls

Post a load, get bids from carriers you have already cleared, and book the one you want. Rates settle in the app and the paperwork follows automatically.

Clearing carriers

Insurance certificates, authority, and safety scores are pulled nightly from the federal registry. A carrier whose authority lapses is pulled from your board before it can bid again, and you get a note explaining which document expired.

Settlement

Payment releases on proof of delivery. Quick-pay carriers take a two percent discount and are funded the same day; everyone else is on net thirty. Both paths reconcile to the same ledger entry, so your accountant sees one number.

Tracking

Location updates come from the carrier's existing telematics provider rather than a driver app nobody installs. If a truck goes dark for more than four hours, the load is flagged and the broker on the account is paged.
