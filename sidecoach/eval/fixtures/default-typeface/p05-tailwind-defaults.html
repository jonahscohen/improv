Kiln fires small-batch ceramics on a shared schedule

Book a shelf instead of a whole kiln. You pay for the volume you use and the firing runs when the load is full.

Scheduling

Bisque runs Tuesday and Friday, glaze runs Wednesday and Saturday. A shelf reserved before noon makes the next run in its cycle; after that it rolls forward one slot. The board shows how full each run is so you can decide whether to wait.

Cones and schedules

We fire to cone 6 oxidation as the house schedule and to cone 10 reduction on the last Saturday of the month. Custom ramps are accepted for a full-kiln booking, and the logged curve is emailed to you after the run.

Breakage

Pieces that fail in the kiln are photographed and logged. If the failure traces to our schedule rather than your construction, the firing is refunded and the shelf is rebooked at no charge.
