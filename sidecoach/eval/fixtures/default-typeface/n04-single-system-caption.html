Halyard rigs traditional sail

We splice and serve running rigging for gaff and square rig, in natural fibre or a modern core with a hemp-coloured cover when class rules allow it.

Materials

Manila and hemp are stocked in three-strand and four-strand up to two inches. Modern cordage is Dyneema-cored with a polyester cover; we can serve and parcel it so it passes a survey that asks for a traditional appearance without giving up the strength.

Splicing

Every eye is hand-spliced with a minimum of five tucks and served over the throat. We do not swage traditional cordage, and we will not certify a splice we did not make, though we are happy to inspect one and tell you plainly what we see.

Survey work

We survey standing and running rigging for insurers on vessels up to a hundred feet. The report lists each line by station with its condition and a replacement interval, priced so an owner can stage the work over two seasons instead of one.
