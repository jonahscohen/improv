Brackish monitors estuary water quality

Sensors report salinity, dissolved oxygen, and turbidity every fifteen minutes. Shellfish leaseholders get an alert before a closure is posted, not after.

The sensor

Each buoy carries a conductivity probe, an optical dissolved-oxygen sensor, and a nephelometer on a shared mast. Power comes from a small panel with a four-day battery reserve, so a stretch of overcast weather does not create a gap in the record.

Closures

State closures are driven by rainfall totals and coliform sampling. Brackish pairs its own readings with the published rainfall gauges so a leaseholder can see a closure coming a day ahead and pull product early instead of losing it.

The record

Every reading is retained at full resolution. You can export a lease's full history as CSV for a permit application, and the export carries the calibration log for each sensor that contributed to it.
