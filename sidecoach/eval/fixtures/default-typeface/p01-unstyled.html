Ledger keeps your books current

Connect a bank account and every transaction lands categorized within the hour.

Reconciliation

Ledger matches each bank line against an invoice or a bill and leaves the unmatched remainder in a queue you can clear in a single pass. Most accounts close the month with fewer than ten open lines.

Filing

Quarterly estimates are drafted from the current ledger and held for your approval. Nothing is filed until you sign it. Federal and state forms are generated from the same numbers, so the two never disagree.
