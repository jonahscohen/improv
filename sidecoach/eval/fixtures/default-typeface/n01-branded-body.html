Alluvium maps river sediment

We survey channel bathymetry twice a year and publish the change so dredging crews know where the material actually moved.

Survey method

A multibeam sounder on a shallow-draft launch covers the navigable channel at half-metre resolution. Positions are corrected against a shore base station, so a survey two seasons apart is comparable to within a few centimetres of vertical.

Deliverables

You get the point cloud, a gridded surface, and a difference raster against the prior survey. The difference raster is the one crews use: it shows deposition in warm colours and scour in cool ones, with the cut volume totalled per reach.

Turnaround

Field work on a ten-mile reach takes two days. Processed deliverables follow within a week, and a preliminary difference surface is available the next morning if a dredge is standing by.
