Tessellate cuts stone to a drawing

Send a DXF and a material and we return a quote with the offcut plan attached, so you can see what the yield actually is before you commit.

Nesting

Parts are nested against the slab you selected, not a nominal rectangle. Veining and any fissure the slab photo shows are treated as no-cut zones, so a part never lands across a flaw you would have rejected on the floor.

Tolerances

Waterjet holds a quarter millimetre on the cut edge and the CNC router holds a tenth on the profile. Anything tighter is ground, quoted separately, and inspected against your drawing with the report attached to the shipment.

Lead time

Stock materials cut within four working days. Slabs we have to source add whatever the quarry quotes, and we pass that date through unchanged rather than padding it.
