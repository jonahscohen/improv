Notes from the coast survey

The tide tables shifted by eleven minutes over the season, and the survey crew logged each reading against the harbor clock before the morning fog rolled in across the flats.

Eastern shelf

By the third week the crew had mapped the whole eastern shelf, marking every sandbar and channel that the earlier charts had left blank or drawn from memory rather than measurement.

The final report ran to forty pages of tables, one row for every station, with the wind and water readings copied out in a clean hand for the county archive to keep.
