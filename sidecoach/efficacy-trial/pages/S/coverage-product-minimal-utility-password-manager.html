Security model

How your passwords stay protected

The short version: your vault is encrypted before it leaves your device, and only you hold the key that unlocks it.

  • Encrypted on your device first

    Your vault is encrypted and decrypted on your own device using AES-256, an industry-standard method for protecting stored data. It's already encrypted by the time anything reaches VaultNest's servers.

  • Your master password never leaves your device

    VaultNest never receives, stores, or transmits your master password. There's no copy of it on our servers to lose or hand over.

  • We can't read your vault

    Because encryption happens on your device, VaultNest staff cannot view your stored passwords, notes, or documents, even if asked to.

  • Two-factor authentication

    Turn on a second sign-in step using an authenticator app or a security key, so a password alone isn't enough to get into your account.

  • Breach and reuse alerts

    VaultNest checks your saved passwords against known weaknesses, reuse across sites, and public breach records, and tells you what to fix first.

What this means if you forget your master password

Because we never store it, we can't reset it for you. During setup, VaultNest gives you a printable recovery kit. Keep it somewhere safe, like a locked drawer, separate from your devices.

Import & setup

Getting started takes a few minutes

Bring your existing passwords with you. Nothing has to be re-typed from scratch.

  1. Install VaultNest

    Add the browser extension and the app for your phone or computer. Both connect to the same vault.

  2. Import your existing passwords

    Bring passwords in directly from Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge, or upload a CSV file exported from another password manager.

  3. Turn on autofill

    Let VaultNest fill logins for you on sites and apps, instead of typing or copy-pasting each one.

  4. Set up two-factor authentication

    Add a second sign-in step to your VaultNest account using an authenticator app or a security key.

What VaultNest can import from

Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge saved passwords, plus CSV exports from most other password managers. During import, you'll see exactly what was brought in before anything is saved to your vault.

Help & support

Help when you need it

Answers to the questions people ask most before and after switching.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Because VaultNest never stores your master password, we can't recover it for you. During setup you'll get a printable recovery kit. Keep it somewhere safe and separate from your devices.

Can I use VaultNest without an internet connection?

Yes. Your vault is stored encrypted on your device, so you can view and use existing passwords offline. Anything you add or change syncs once you're back online.

What happens to shared vaults if someone leaves my team?

Removing someone from your team revokes their access to shared vaults right away. Items in their personal vault stay private to them and aren't affected.

Which browsers and platforms are supported?

VaultNest works with Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge, plus apps for iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac.

How do I cancel my plan?

Cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll keep Premium or Team features until the end of your current billing period.
