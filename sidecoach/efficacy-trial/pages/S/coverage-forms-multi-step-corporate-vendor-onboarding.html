Before you begin

This application has six short sections. Most applicants finish in about 15 minutes, though you may want a few documents on hand from your finance and compliance teams before you start.

  1. Company profile - legal name, entity type, and business address.
  2. Primary contact - the person we should reach about this application.
  3. Compliance attestations - confirmations required for supplier approval.
  4. Tax & payment details - tax classification and banking information for payment setup.
  5. Document uploads - tax form, insurance certificate, and business license.
  6. Review - confirm everything before you submit.
