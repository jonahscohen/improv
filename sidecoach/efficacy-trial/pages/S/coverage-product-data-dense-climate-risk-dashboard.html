Example portfolio questions the product answers

These are the questions analysts bring to SiteSignal Risk during quarterly reviews and acquisition diligence.

Which assets cross our underwriting flood threshold under the 2050 scenario, and how has that changed since acquisition?
Ranked by fluvial, pluvial, and coastal exposure at the parcel level, compared against the position at acquisition.
How much of the industrial portfolio sits inside a high wildfire-hazard zone, weighted by net operating income?
NOI-weighted rollups by fund, region, or asset type.
Which three markets show the steepest rise in heat-index days over the next fifteen years?
Decadal heat-day frequency trends compared across held and target markets.
What share of the coastal book is exposed to inundation under the 2100 sea-level scenario, before and after planned retrofits?
Side-by-side scenario comparison at the asset and portfolio level.
How does current wind exposure compare to the position we held at last year's disclosure cycle?
Year-over-year exposure delta, aligned to your prior TCFD or ISSB filing.
Which five assets should move to the top of the resilience-retrofit list this quarter?
A prioritized list scored against aggregate exposure and estimated remediation cost.
