Community clinic, open to everyone

You're welcome here, whatever your situation.

Harbor Street Health offers affordable primary care and support services to people who live nearby. Insured or not, documented or not, first visit or tenth — there is a way for you to get care here.

Services offered

Harbor Street Health is a full primary care clinic. Most residents can get everything they need for everyday health right here, without a referral to start.

Primary & preventive care

Checkups, physicals, and ongoing care from a doctor or nurse practitioner who gets to know you over time.

Same-day sick visits

Cold, flu, infection, injury — we hold same-day slots every morning for anyone who needs to be seen that day.

Chronic condition care

Ongoing management for diabetes, high blood pressure, asthma, and other conditions that need regular check-ins.

Vaccinations & screenings

Routine immunizations, blood pressure and cholesterol checks, cancer screenings, and other preventive tests.

Behavioral health support

Counseling and support for stress, anxiety, depression, and other concerns, with same-clinic referrals when more help is needed.

Specialist referrals

When you need care beyond what we offer, we connect you directly to a specialist and help you understand the next step.

Eligibility and payment

No one is turned away from Harbor Street Health because they can't pay. Here's how cost and eligibility actually work.

Sliding-scale fees
Your visit cost is based on household income and size. Many residents pay a small flat fee per visit; some pay nothing. We calculate this with you at check-in, not before.
Insurance accepted
Medicaid, Medicare, and most private insurance plans. Bring your card if you have one, but you do not need insurance to be seen.
Uninsured residents
You are welcome here. Discounted self-pay rates apply automatically, and financial assistance can lower the cost further.
Immigration status
We do not ask about immigration status, and it never affects the care you receive or what you are charged.
Financial assistance
Ask the front desk for a financial assistance application at any visit. Approval can apply retroactively to that day's care.

Appointment options

There are a few ways to get on the schedule, depending on how soon you need to be seen.

Call to schedule

Phone (555) 555-0142, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. This is the fastest way to book a routine visit.

Same-day sick visits

Call as early in the day as possible. We hold a limited number of same-day slots each morning for urgent, non-emergency needs.

New patient visits

Your first appointment runs about 45 minutes so we can properly get to know your health history. Returning visits are usually 20 minutes.

Walk-in guidance

Prefer not to call ahead, or don't have a phone handy? You can walk in.

When can I walk in?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Walk-ins are seen in the order they arrive, after anyone with a scheduled appointment at that time.

How long is the wait?

Typically 20 to 60 minutes, longer on Monday mornings. Front desk staff can give you a realistic estimate when you arrive, and you're welcome to leave and come back.

Should I call ahead instead?

If you know you need a specific test, a longer visit, or an interpreter for a language other than Spanish, calling ahead helps us prepare. For everything else, walking in works just as well.

Language support

Care is available in your language, whether or not our front-desk staff speaks it.

Front-desk and clinical staff speak English and Spanish on-site every day. For every other language, we connect you with a free phone interpreter during your visit — just let us know when you arrive or when you call.

What to bring

Bring what you have — nothing on this list is required to be seen.

  • 1 Insurance card, if you have one. If not, that's fine — just let us know.
  • 2 A list of current medications, or the pill bottles themselves.
  • 3 Any recent medical records or test results, if you have access to them.
  • 4 A photo ID, if you have one. It is not required to receive care.
  • 5 Questions written down in advance, so nothing gets forgotten in the visit.
