Bike share for the neighborhood

Grab a bike. Go where you're going.

LoopLane puts a bike within a five-minute walk of most homes, offices, and transit stops downtown. Unlock one with your phone, ride to where you're headed, and dock it at any station along the way.

How it works

Three steps, no experience required

  1. 01

    Find a bike

    Look for a green LoopLane dock. Most neighborhoods have one within a five-minute walk.

  2. 02

    Unlock in seconds

    Scan the code on the bike with your phone's camera. No app install or account needed for your first ride.

  3. 03

    Ride and return

    Pedal to where you're headed and dock the bike at any LoopLane station in the coverage area. Your ride ends the moment it locks in.

Safety and support

Ride prepared, get help fast

  • Every bike ships with front and rear lights that turn on automatically after dark.
  • Helmets aren't provided, but wearing one is strongly recommended, and required by law for riders under 18.
  • Follow the same traffic laws as any vehicle: ride with traffic, signal turns, and use bike lanes where they exist.
  • Report a damaged bike or a station issue from the lock screen. Our team responds within the hour, every day.
  • Reach support by phone or text from 6am to midnight, every day. Overnight messages get a callback the next morning.
