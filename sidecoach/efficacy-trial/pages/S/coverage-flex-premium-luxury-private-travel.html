Our promise

Most travel planning breaks down at the seams: a beautiful hotel booked next to a transfer that does not exist, a "custom" itinerary assembled from the same six templates every other agency uses. Meridian starts from your actual constraints and preferences, then designs the trip around them.

You work with one advisor who stays with you from the first conversation through your return home. They coordinate flights, lodging, private guides, ground transportation, and the small logistics that usually fall through the cracks, so the plan reads as one coherent trip rather than a stack of confirmation emails.

How planning works

Four stages, the same for every trip regardless of complexity.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A conversation about how you actually travel: pace, budget range, non-negotiables, and what has gone wrong on past trips.

  2. 02

    Itinerary architecture

    Your advisor builds a day-by-day structure first, then fills it with specific properties, guides, and transfers.

  3. 03

    Refinement and confirmation

    You review, adjust, and approve. Bookings are only confirmed once the full itinerary is locked, not piece by piece.

  4. 04

    In-trip support

    A direct line to your advisor for the duration of the trip, for anything from a missed connection to a last-minute change of plan.

How working together works

Two ways to engage an advisor, depending on how often you travel with us.

Single-Journey Planning Per trip

A complimentary discovery call, followed by a scoped planning fee based on trip length and complexity, discussed plainly before any work begins.

  • One advisor for the full trip lifecycle
  • Planning fee confirmed after discovery call
  • In-trip support included

Atelier Membership Annual

For clients who travel with us more than once a year. An annual arrangement that keeps the same advisor across trips and prioritizes your itinerary requests.

  • Continuity with one advisor year over year
  • Priority scheduling during peak booking windows
  • Reduced planning fees on repeat itineraries

Exact fees and membership terms are set during your discovery call, once we understand the shape of your travel. We do not quote a number before that conversation happens.

Client service standards

What you can expect from every advisor, on every trip.

  • One advisor, start to finish. The person on your discovery call is the person who plans, confirms, and supports your trip.

  • A staffed support line while you travel. Reached directly, not through a shared queue or an outsourced call center.

  • One business day response. For planning inquiries before a trip is underway.

  • Full documentation, delivered plainly. A complete itinerary in digital and printed form, with every confirmation number in one place.

  • Direct vetting. Every property, guide, and operator we recommend has been visited or used firsthand by our team.

  • Honest limits. If a request is not realistic for the timeline or destination, we say so during planning, not after you have arrived.
