The compliance problem

Evidence lives in six systems. Auditors only care about one answer.

Freight security audits touch carrier credentialing, driver vetting, cargo custody logs, and incident records - and at most mid-sized carriers, that evidence is scattered across email threads, shared drives, and disconnected point tools.

When a regulator, insurer, or shipper partner asks for proof, compliance teams spend days reconstructing a paper trail instead of minutes retrieving one. Gaps in that trail translate directly into audit findings, higher insurance premiums, and lost shipper confidence.

Integration overview

Fits into the stack you already run

CargoProof sits alongside your TMS and ELD rather than replacing them, syncing the compliance-relevant data automatically.

  • TMS synchronization

    Two-way sync with common transportation management systems for load, carrier, and lane data.

    Pull custody-relevant location and hours-of-service events directly from connected ELD providers.
