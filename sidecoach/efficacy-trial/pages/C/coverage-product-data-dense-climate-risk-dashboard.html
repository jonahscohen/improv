00 / Overview

What SiteSignal Risk is built to answer

A reference layer for physical risk, not a live monitoring feed - designed to be interpretable on its own, without a connection to real-time data sources.

Coverage

Eight federal, scientific, and industry datasets are normalized into a single asset-level risk record, updated on a published, versioned schedule rather than streamed in real time.

Application

Used to screen acquisitions, prioritize insurance and capital planning, and produce disclosure-ready exports for portfolios ranging from a single asset to national holdings.

Interpretation

Every score, chart, and export carries its source dataset and vintage, so a reviewer can trace a number back to its origin without needing a live system connection.

05 / Refresh Schedule

Data refresh cadence

Refresh frequency is set by each source agency's own publication schedule.

Monthly

Wildfire perimeters (fire season), Storm Events Database, Drought Monitor ingestion.

Quarterly

National Flood Hazard Layer revisions, wildfire perimeters (off-season).

Semiannual

Local elevation certificate and building code registry updates, where jurisdictions publish them.

Annual

Sea Level Rise Viewer projections, CMIP6 heat model re-run, aggregated industry loss data.

Every refresh is versioned. Portfolio reports and dashboard views display the coverage vintage used for that analysis, so results remain interpretable after the fact even without an active connection to the source systems.
