Team formation

Find your team, or bring one

Most attendees show up without a full team. That is normal, and Friday is built around it.

Register as a team

Already have 2 to 5 people? Everyone registers individually and lists the same team name. You start building Friday night.

Register solo

You'll fill out a short interest form and get matched into a team at Friday's mixer, based on skills and the kind of problem you want to work on.

Register with one teammate

Come as a pair and grow your team to full size at the mixer, or stay a duo. Both are common and both work fine.

No team is too small or too mismatched. Teams that mix majors consistently do best, since every project needs people who think differently about the same problem.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience to join?+

No. Roughly half of BuildBurst attendees have never done anything like this before. Every workshop and mentor is built with that in mind.

I don't have a team. Can I still come?+

Yes, most people don't. Friday's mixer exists specifically to match solo students into teams before building starts.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. Meals, snacks, and materials are included for every registered, currently enrolled student.

What should I bring?+

A laptop if you have one, though it isn't required for every role on a team. Bring a phone charger and comfortable clothes. We provide everything else.

Can first-year students join?+

Yes. Every year and every major is welcome, including first-years and incoming transfer students.

I have class Friday afternoon. Can I still participate?+

Yes. Check-in runs from 5 to 6 PM and the core building happens Saturday and Sunday. Arriving right at 5 works fine.

What if my idea doesn't go anywhere by Sunday?+

Most ideas don't turn into a finished product in one weekend, and that's expected. The goal is the experience, the team, and the skills, not a launched company.

Is there a registration deadline?+

Register by October 9, 2026. Spots are limited by venue capacity, so earlier is safer.
