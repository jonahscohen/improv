Services we offer

Harbor Street Health is a primary care clinic. Most residents come to us for the care below - if you're not sure your need fits, call us and we'll help you figure out the right next step.

Primary and preventive care

Check-ups, vaccinations, screenings, and ongoing care for chronic conditions like diabetes or high blood pressure.

Same-day sick visits

Colds, flu, infections, minor injuries, and other things that can't wait for a scheduled appointment.

Women's and family health

Well-woman exams, prenatal check-ins, and family planning support.

Behavioral health support

Short-term counseling and referrals for anxiety, stress, and other mental health needs.

Medication management

Prescription refills, reviews, and help understanding how to take your medications.

Referrals & care navigation

If you need a specialist or a service we don't provide, our staff will help you find and connect with one.

Eligibility & payment

Cost and paperwork stop a lot of people from getting care they need. Here's the honest, plain version of how it works at Harbor Street Health.

You will not be turned away for lack of insurance or money.

  • No insurance is required. We serve uninsured and underinsured residents every day.
  • Sliding-scale fees are based on household size and income, so you pay what you can reasonably afford.
  • Medicaid, Medicare, and most major insurance plans are accepted. Bring your card if you have one, but don't worry if you're not sure it's accepted - we'll check for you.
  • Payment plans are available for any remaining balance. Ask the front desk about setting one up.

If you're in serious pain, having trouble breathing, or think this may be an emergency, please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room instead of waiting for a walk-in slot.

Language support

You have the right to be understood, and we won't let language be a barrier to your care. Free interpreter services are available for every visit - just let us know when you call or arrive.

What to bring

Bring what you have - nothing on this list is required to be seen. If you're missing something, tell the front desk and we'll work with you.

  • Photo ID If you have one A driver's license, state ID, or passport helps us find your record faster, but you can still be seen without one.
  • Insurance card If you have one If you have Medicaid, Medicare, or private insurance, bring the card. If not, just let us know - sliding-scale pricing is available.
  • A list of current medications Names and dosages, or the bottles themselves, help our providers understand your health picture.
  • Proof of income Only for sliding-scale pricing A recent pay stub or benefits letter helps us set your sliding-scale rate. If you don't have this yet, we can still see you and sort it out afterward.
  • Someone to help translate Optional You're welcome to bring a family member or friend, but it's not required - free interpreters are available on request.
