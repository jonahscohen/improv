A sample recipe from the kit

One of six recipes included in every box - here's what a typical cooking session looks like.

Rainbow Veggie Quesadillas

Prep time: 15 min Serves: 2 tiny chefs No-heat option available

Kids measure and layer three colors of veggies with cheese inside a folded tortilla, then either press it cold for a no-cook version or ask an adult to warm it in a pan for a crisp, melty finish.

  1. Measure and rinse the veggies using the color-coded measuring cups.
  2. Use the safety knife to cut soft veggies into strips.
  3. Layer veggies and cheese onto the tortilla and fold.
  4. Press or pan-warm the quesadilla until golden.Adult step
  5. Slice into wedges and plate it up.

Common questions

Do I need to supervise the whole time?

For ages 6–7, yes, stay nearby throughout. For ages 8–10, most kids can complete the no-heat steps independently and just need you for the flagged stovetop step.

What if my child has a food allergy?

Every recipe card lists allergens up front (dairy, gluten, and eggs appear across the six recipes) so you can swap ingredients before you start.

Can I cancel the Recipe Club subscription anytime?

Yes - pause, skip a month, or cancel from your account at any time with no fee.

Is the knife actually safe for a 6-year-old?

The included knife is a rounded-tip, serrated bamboo tool made to slice soft produce like bananas and cooked veggies. It will not cut skin, but we still recommend supervision during all knife steps.
