The Meridian Promise

We design one journey, for one client, and refine it until it is right, then stand behind it for as long as you are travelling.

Meridian exists for travelers whose itineraries cannot be assembled from a search bar. We build journeys around access, timing, and detail that come from a dedicated advisor with standing relationships across the destinations we serve: private guides, small hotels with no public allotment, restaurants that do not take walk-ins, and operators who answer our calls first.

We do not publish packages, and we do not promise outcomes we cannot guarantee. What we promise is attentive, expert planning; realistic expectations set early; and a single point of contact from your first conversation with us to the day you return home.

How a Journey Comes Together

A planning process built on conversation, not checkboxes

Most engagements move through the following stages. Timelines vary with the complexity of the journey and how far in advance you begin planning.

01

Introductory consultation

A private conversation to understand your travel history, priorities, and non-negotiables. No itinerary is proposed at this stage.

02

Proposal & concept

Your advisor returns with a considered itinerary concept built specifically for your trip, along with an estimated range of investment.

03

Refinement

The itinerary is adjusted through direct conversation, covering pacing, accommodations, and experiences, until every detail reflects your expectations.

04

Confirmation & documentation

Bookings, travel documentation, and logistics are confirmed on your behalf, with a written itinerary and contingency notes provided ahead of departure.

05

In-journey support

A direct line to your advisor and vetted on-the-ground contacts for the duration of travel, should plans need to change.

06

Return & reflection

A short follow-up conversation after you return, to record preferences that inform how we plan your next journey.

Engagement & Membership

Two ways to work with Meridian

Every relationship begins with a consultation. From there, most clients choose one of the following arrangements, discussed and confirmed before any planning fee is charged.

Journey Engagement

Per Trip

A single, dedicated engagement for one upcoming journey, from initial concept through your return.

  • One advisor assigned for the full planning cycle
  • Itinerary concept, refinement, and documentation
  • In-journey support for the duration of travel
  • Planning fee confirmed in writing before work begins

Annual Membership

Ongoing

Standing access to your advisor across the year, for clients who travel privately several times per year.

  • Everything in Journey Engagement, for multiple trips
  • A standing preference profile that carries between journeys
  • Priority scheduling during high-demand travel windows
  • Annual planning conversation to review upcoming travel

Meridian does not publish fixed pricing for either arrangement, as the appropriate structure depends on the complexity and frequency of your travel. Specifics are discussed plainly during your consultation, and no planning fee is charged before you have agreed to it.

Client Service Standards

What we hold ourselves to

A single point of contact

One advisor is assigned to your journey and remains your point of contact throughout, with no hand-offs between departments.

Confidentiality as standard practice

Client details, itineraries, and preferences are treated as confidential by default, not as an exception granted on request.

A vetted partner network

Guides, drivers, and properties are selected from relationships Meridian maintains directly, reviewed on an ongoing basis.

Direct availability while traveling

A reachable line to your advisor or an on-the-ground contact for the duration of your trip, not a shared support queue.

Transparent fees, agreed in advance

Planning fees are discussed and confirmed in writing before any work begins, with no undisclosed markups added later.

Contingency built into every itinerary

Each journey includes a plan for foreseeable disruptions, such as weather, closures, or changes of mind, reviewed with you before departure.
