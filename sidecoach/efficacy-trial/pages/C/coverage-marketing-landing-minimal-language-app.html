Inside a lesson

What today's habit actually looks like

No surprises before you start a trial. Here is the full shape of a single Tuesday lesson, described step by step.

  1. 1

    Warm-up recall

    2 min

    You revisit yesterday's three phrases with quick recall prompts, so nothing is learned once and forgotten.

  2. 2

    New phrase

    3 min

    You learn a natural way to greet a coworker and ask about their weekend, with a plain-language breakdown of each word.

  3. 3

    Listen and repeat

    2 min

    You hear the phrase spoken at a natural pace, then repeat it aloud twice to build muscle memory for the sound.

  4. 4

    Real-life scenario

    2 min

    You choose how you would respond in a short workplace conversation, using the phrase you just learned.

  5. 5

    Wrap-up

    1 min

    A one-line recap and a preview of tomorrow's phrase, so tomorrow's ten minutes start already warmed up.
