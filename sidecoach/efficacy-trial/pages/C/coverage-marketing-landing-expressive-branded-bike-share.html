How it works

Three steps, no learning curve

New to bike share? LoopLane is built so your first ride feels as easy as your fiftieth.

1

Find a bike

Check any station map for the nearest available bike, or use the free LoopLane app to see live counts across the coverage area.

2

Unlock and ride

Scan the code on the bike or tap your LoopLane card at the dock. No app account needed at the station — just scan and go.

3

Dock it and done

Return the bike to any station in the coverage area. You're only charged for the time you actually rode, down to the minute.

Safety & support

Built to feel safe for a first ride

Every bike is inspected regularly, and help is never more than a tap or a call away.

1

Bikes checked daily

Staff inspect brakes, tires, and lights across every station each day, and any reported issue locks that bike from further use until it's serviced.

2

Lit for every ride

All bikes have front and rear lights that switch on automatically at dusk, plus reflective decals so you're visible after dark.

3

Report in one tap

See a problem bike? Flag it from the app or the number on the frame and it's pulled from service before the next rider unlocks it.
