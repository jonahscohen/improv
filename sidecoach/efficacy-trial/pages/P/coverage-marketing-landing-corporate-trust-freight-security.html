The compliance problem

The compliance blind spot most carriers can’t see coming

Security and compliance records for most carriers live in three or four places at once — spreadsheets, a TMS, a shared drive, a compliance lead’s inbox. That works until an audit, an incident, or a shipper security review asks for a complete answer on short notice.

Fragmented records

No single source of truth

Chain-of-custody, driver vetting, and incident history are scattered across systems that were never built to talk to each other.

Reactive audit prep

Audits become a scramble

Preparing for a DOT review or a shipper security assessment means pulling records manually, under deadline, with no guarantee they’re complete.

Inconsistent vetting

Vendor risk goes unmonitored

Carrier and broker credentials are checked once at onboarding and rarely revisited, leaving blind spots that surface only when something goes wrong.

Security & data handling

Assurances built into the platform, not bolted on

CargoProof is built to meet the security expectations that shippers, auditors, and procurement teams bring to a vendor review.

Encryption everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest across every environment the platform runs in.

Role-based access control

Access follows least-privilege defaults, with permissions scoped to role, facility, and lane.

Full activity logging

Every user and system action is logged, so who accessed or changed a record is never in question.

Data residency & retention

Retention periods and storage location are configurable to match your regulatory and contractual obligations.
