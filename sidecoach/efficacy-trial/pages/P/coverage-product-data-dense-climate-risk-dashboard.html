Product Overview

Physical climate risk analytics for real estate portfolios, priced and reported at the asset level.

SiteSignal Risk quantifies flood, wildfire, extreme heat, sea level rise, wind, and water stress exposure across a portfolio, tying each score to underlying public and licensed datasets so analysts and asset managers can defend every number in an investment committee or lender conversation.

Risk categories

Six physical risk categories are scored per asset on a 1–100 index, each backed by a stated methodology and projection horizon.

6 categories

Flood Risk

Riverine and pluvial flood exposure derived from FEMA flood zones plus first-floor elevation where available.

Horizon30 yr
Primary inputFEMA NFHL

Wildfire Exposure

Structure ignition potential from wildland-urban interface proximity and fuel load modeling.

Horizon10–20 yr
Primary inputUSGS WHP

Extreme Heat

Projected days above regional heat thresholds and implications for cooling load and tenant comfort.

Horizon2040 / 2060
Primary inputNOAA / NASA NEX

Sea Level Rise & Coastal Inundation

Chronic and episodic coastal flooding exposure under multiple sea level rise scenarios.

Horizon2050 / 2100
Primary inputNOAA SLR Viewer

Hurricane & Wind

Peak gust probability and storm surge co-exposure for coastal and near-coastal assets.

HorizonAnnualized
Primary inputNOAA SLOSH

Water Stress & Drought

Baseline water depletion and drought risk affecting operating cost and irrigation-dependent assets.

Horizon2030
Primary inputWRI Aqueduct

Example portfolio questions this product answers

Each question maps to a saved analysis template. Expand a question to see which datasets and risk categories back the answer.

6 templates
Which assets fall within a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area, and how did that change under the latest map revision?

Compares current and prior FEMA NFHL vintages against the asset address book, flagging newly designated or de-designated parcels and the resulting change in flood risk index.

Flood Risk
FEMA NFHL, parcel records
What share of assets under management face high or severe wildfire hazard potential within 10 years?

Rolls up parcel-level Wildfire Hazard Potential scores to fund and portfolio level, weighted by asset value, and segments the result by market and sub-portfolio.

Wildfire Exposure
USGS WHP, parcel records
Which markets show the steepest projected rise in extreme heat days by 2040, and what does that mean for capex planning?

Ranks covered metro markets by projected change in days above the regional heat threshold and cross-references reported HVAC system age where available.

Extreme Heat
NOAA / NASA NEX projections
How exposed is a target acquisition to sea level rise under a 1-meter rise scenario by 2100?

Runs a single-asset scenario comparison across 0.5m, 1m, and 2m sea level rise projections against the NOAA coastal elevation model, output as a due-diligence one-pager.

Sea Level Rise
NOAA SLR Viewer
Which regional sub-portfolios have the highest concentration of water-stressed assets?

Joins Aqueduct basin-level water stress scores to the asset book and correlates concentration against reported tenant industry mix for operational sensitivity.

Water Stress
WRI Aqueduct
Where do climate risk scores diverge most from current insurance premiums?

Compares the composite asset risk index against client-supplied premium data to surface assets that may be mispriced relative to modeled physical risk.

Composite Index
All categories, loss history
