What a daily lesson looks like

Every lesson follows the same calm structure, so there is nothing new to figure out each morning.

Day 12 · Ordering coffee

"Un café con leche, por favor."

Translation: "A coffee with milk, please."

A coffee with milk, please.
A tea with sugar, please.
The check, please.

Three minutes of listening. Three minutes of speaking. Four minutes of review.

  • You hear a short phrase spoken at a natural pace, then repeat it aloud.
  • A quick multiple-choice check confirms you understood the meaning, not just the sound.
  • The lesson closes with two or three phrases from earlier days, timed to keep them fresh.
  • A short note explains one grammar point in plain language, only when it is useful.
