How We Work

The planning process

A typical engagement moves through six stages. Complex itineraries move through them more than once as details firm up.

  1. Discovery Consultation

    A conversation, not a questionnaire. We learn who is traveling, what has worked or fallen flat before, and what this particular trip needs to accomplish.

  2. Curated Proposal

    Your advisor returns with a specific itinerary: properties, pacing, and experiences named, not categories left blank, along with a transparent cost outline.

  3. Refinement

    We adjust against your feedback until the shape of the trip matches what you had in mind, including changes of scope, not just swapped hotels.

  4. Confirmation & Documentation

    Bookings are secured, entry and health requirements are confirmed, and you receive a single consolidated itinerary rather than a stack of separate confirmations.

  5. In-Trip Support

    Your advisor and our on-the-ground network remain reachable for the duration of travel, so a delayed flight or a closed road is our problem to solve, not yours.

  6. Post-Trip Follow-Up

    We review what worked, note preferences for next time, and keep your travel profile current for the trip after this one.

Membership & Inquiry

How the relationship works

Engagement model

  • New relationships begin with a private consultation, arranged directly or through an existing client's introduction.
  • Most clients engage us per trip; an ongoing advisory arrangement is available for households traveling several times a year.
  • Planning fees are disclosed before work begins and are separate from the cost of the trip itself.
  • There is no public tier list or point system; your advisor scopes the arrangement to the trips you actually take.

What we ask in return

  • Enough lead time to secure access that cannot be arranged on short notice, typically eight or more weeks for complex itineraries.
  • Honest feedback during the proposal stage, so the plan reflects what you actually want rather than what was asked first.
  • A single point of contact on your side for decisions, to keep changes from crossing in transit.
Client Service Standards

What we hold ourselves to

A named advisor, not a queue

You reach the same person every time, and that person carries the full history of your preferences from trip to trip.

Reachable while you travel

Support during your trip is available at any hour, through a direct line your advisor gives you at confirmation.

A vetted partner network

Properties and guides are chosen from relationships we maintain and revisit, not from a rotating commission list.

Discretion as a default

Client details, travel dates, and itineraries are held in confidence and are never shared or referenced without permission.

Plain answers on cost

Proposals separate planning fees from trip cost, and we say clearly when something is outside what we can arrange.

Honest limits

We tell you when a request is unrealistic for the timeline or destination rather than agreeing and hoping it works out.
