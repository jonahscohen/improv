New to bike share? Start here

Three steps. No experience needed.

You don't need an account history with bike share to use LoopLane. If you can use a phone and ride a bike, you're set.

1

Find a bike nearby

Open the LoopLane app or walk up to any station. Live availability shows exactly which docks have a bike ready to go.

2

Unlock and ride

Scan the code on the bike or tap your membership card. The lock releases in seconds and you're rolling.

3

Dock it and go

Slide the bike into any open dock across the coverage area. Your ride ends automatically and your receipt lands in the app.

Safety and support

Built so first-time riders feel confident, fast.

Every bike is inspected regularly and every rider gets the same safety basics up front.

Daily bike checks

Brakes, tires, and lights are inspected on a rolling schedule. Anything flagged is pulled from service the same day.

Free helmet fitting

Stop by any LoopLane hub for a free helmet fitting and a five-minute walkthrough of hand signals and local bike lanes.

Common questions

New to bike share? These usually come up first.

Do I need to own a bike helmet?+
No. Free helmet fittings are available at every LoopLane hub, and loaner helmets are available at major stations.
What happens if every dock near me is full?+
The app shows live dock space and gives you 10 extra minutes free to reach the next nearest station, no charge.
Can I try it before committing to a membership?+
Yes. Your very first ride is free, and pay-per-ride pricing has no sign-up commitment at all.
Is LoopLane available outside the main coverage area?+
Coverage currently focuses on downtown, the university district, and the riverside path, with new stations added quarterly.
